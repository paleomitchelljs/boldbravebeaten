Combat Arena Results

Sixteen level-5 fighters (four armor classes × four fighting styles) fought every other fighter in an open field and in close quarters. This round adds a longbow draw, no attacks inside a weapon's reach (with a Shove action), power swings that break and push through shields, and a lighter plate penalty.

Every fight is played by the built-in AI. It keeps its preferred range, kites with bows and spells, winds up and draws, shoves to regain distance, swaps between weapon and dagger, and defends by the odds, but it never retreats to heal, flanks, or plans across rounds. Read these as what the rules produce under competent, simple play. Bows and magic are placeholder rules.

For the designer

Where the rules need work

This roundWhat the changes did

  • Plate retune (+1 stamina per action instead of +2, −3 regeneration instead of −4): heavy armor went from 44% to 52% in the open and 49% to 62% in close quarters. Collapses fell from 0.51 to 0.41 a fight in the open and 0.48 to 0.33 in close quarters. Heavy armor is now best where your Philosophy says it should be, confined melee.
  • No attacks inside reach, plus Shove: two-handers in close quarters rose from 42% to 60%; heavy two-handers from 43% to 80%. A greatsword now holds its 2–3 band instead of doing half damage at 1.
  • Power swings vs shields: in close quarters, two-handers against sword & shield rose from 14% to 31%. Blocked power blows break about 0.1–0.13 shields a fight and drive excess force through.
  • Longbow draw: archers fell from 63% to 59% in the open and from 50% to 44% in close quarters. Against two-handers in close quarters they dropped from 48% to 20%.
  • Faster shield wear changes nothing measurable. Fights end before shields wear out; the material-break rule is what matters.

DecisionSword & shield still dominates, because nothing keeps a dagger out

Sword & shield averages 68% in the open and 70% in close quarters. Its fighters step inside a longer weapon's reach and draw a dagger (about 0.9 times a fight), where the shield blocks and the dagger works. Shove can't hold the distance: it is used only about 0.04 times a fight, because re-closing costs the shoved fighter 1 AP for a square.

  • Halving movement (back to the Specifics rate) cuts sword & shield to 55% in the open, but archers rise to 65%. Close quarters doesn't change.
  • So the open question is a rule, not a number: how should a reach weapon keep a shorter one out, given there are no attacks of opportunity?

Options: a "set" or stop-thrust (a reach weapon held ready gets one strike at anyone entering its band, a narrow attack of opportunity); stepping into an enemy's reach costs extra AP; a successful shove also costs the target AP (off balance); a dagger in the off hand can't be used with a shield.

NumbersFights got longer

Average fight length rose from 3.7 to 5.8 rounds in the open and from 2.9 to 4.2 in close quarters. Mages run longest (about 8 rounds in the open): they kite and cast little. Melee-only fights went from 3.6 to 5.0 rounds, because plate now lasts longer. "Fights end quickly at every level" is strained in the open, though group fights may differ.

DeferredMagic is weaker still

Casters average 23% in the open and 17% in close quarters, down from 32% and 25%. Opponents now survive longer (plate) and close the distance better, and the magic stub is unchanged. Left for a dedicated pass, as agreed. Its role may be clearer in group and monster fights.

ContextOne-on-one is only part of the picture

These are duels. Group fights (many against one, a monster against heroes, mixed parties) may favor styles that look weak here: archers behind a shield line, mages with time to prepare, two-handers hitting whatever a shield wall holds. A group arena is the natural next test.

WorkingWhat looks right

  • Ground matters: two-handers and heavy armor are strongest in close quarters; archers and the unarmored are strongest in the open.
  • The wind-up and draw read as intended: exposed, telegraphed, and decisive when they land.
  • Armor ordering in melee is closer to your design: heavy armor leads in close quarters.
