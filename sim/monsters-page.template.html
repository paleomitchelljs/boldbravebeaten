Monster Arena Trials

Seven monsters and a boss, each built to beat one kind of fighter, fought every level-5 arena fighter (four armor classes × two-handed, sword & shield, grappler, archery) one-on-one in an open field and in close quarters, then fought two parties of three.

Verdicts

Did each monster beat what it was built to beat?

Fighters differ a lot on their own (plate wins about 70% against people, the unarmored about 34%), so raw win rates mislead. The edge is how much better the monster does against its target than its own average, compared with how much better the other monsters do against the same target. Positive means the design works.

For the designer

What the trials say

Biggest findingNumbers beat any single monster

A lone monster against three level-5 fighters loses almost every time, even the cave troll that wins 78–87% one-on-one: three times the actions lands three times the wounds. Only a group (the wolf pack) or a boss with its actions multiplied competes with a party. A troll needed about 2.5× AP, per-pass allowance and stamina, plus CON 15, to win 23–50% against a party.

WorkingCounters that do their job

  • Rust beetle is the clearest success (edge +20 / +26): two or three touches turn plate to scrap, then the mandibles work. Against leather it is weak, as intended.
  • Giant spider nearly hits its target (+13 / +10): venom only flows when a bite draws blood, and how much depends on how deep it went, so it beats bare skin and bounces off plate.
  • Owlbear works in the open (+15) but in close quarters it beats nearly everyone (82% against two-handers, 83% against archers), so its edge there shrinks to +2. Sword & shield handles it in both arenas.

Needs workCounters that don't stand out yet

  • Displacer beast is a fair fight overall but only mildly anti-archer: arena archers drop the bow for a dagger the moment anything fast closes in, so the displacement against arrows rarely matters. It needs a reason for archers to keep shooting, or archers that kite better.
  • Cave troll breaks shields as designed, but it beats everyone, so it isn't specifically a shield-breaker. Its natural place is a boss (see the chieftain), or it should lose something (regeneration, reach) to be a mid-tier counter.
  • Lone wolf wins 38–50%: about right for an animal against a trained fighter, maybe a little strong in close quarters.

New rulesWhat the monsters added to the engine

  • Natural weapons (always ready, can't be dropped or disarmed) and natural armor (fur, chitin, troll hide; not lootable).
  • Traits: knockdown bites, pack bonus (+1 damage per packmate beside the target, up to 2), venom (poison a bandage can't stop), rust (corrodes metal armor and shields), displacement (attacks may hit an illusion), shield-crushing blows, regeneration (unless burned; a downed troll can get back up), and boss multipliers for AP, per-pass allowance and stamina.
