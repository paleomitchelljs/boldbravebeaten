# The orc valley

From the user, 2026-11-08: "a new area attached to East Tarnhold ... a valley with an orc fortress & orc mines", with
their hand-drawn map of the classic orc valley and its key, quoted:

> 1 Camps inhabited by Legionnaires
> 2 Castle Crush, where Emperor, Lord Dvinn, Emissaries, the Prophet, and most high levels spawn
> 3 Slave Cabin, with inhabitants needed for Screaming Mace quest
> 4 Slaver Caves, with slaves outside
> 5 Slave Pits with gnome
> 6 Trainer Hill ("Where camping was born...")
> Note that the above map is in error, there is a land connection along the back wall from area 1 to behind the
> castle at area 5

The source's proper names are left out (as with the fire-goblin mine): the emperor, the warlord, the emissaries and
the prophet are unnamed. Ask the user whether they want the names.
