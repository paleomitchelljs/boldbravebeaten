# assets/: the scratch pad

Where art starts. Nothing here is loaded by the game or published; finished art moves to `art/` (see `art/README.md`).

| Folder | What | In git |
|---|---|---|
| `ideas/` | Notes, sketches, reference, mood boards, lists of art we want | yes |
| `packs/` | Packs as bought or downloaded, untouched (Cute Fantasy Dungeons, Volcano, Desert, ShroomLands; Pixel Crawler; Tiny Swords; a CraftPix swamp set), each with its own licence file | no: third-party, and most may not be redistributed |
| `wip/` | Work cut or converted from packs and not yet used: `cliffs/` (outdoor cliff pieces cut from Cute Fantasy), `temple-tileset/` (a 64-px PixelLab wang tileset) | only what may be redistributed; both current folders are git-ignored |

The repo is public: before committing anything here, check its licence allows redistribution. When in doubt, add it to
`.gitignore`.
